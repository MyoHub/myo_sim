Shoulder geometry and data from Seth et al. 2019

The following data in this package are derived from the work below, together with the values computed from them in
the arm, torso and head files:
  - scapulothoracic.json: the scapulothoracic geometry (thoracic ellipsoid, scapular joint point, acromioclavicular
    points, sternoclavicular axes), the girdle's rest posture and ranges, and the rhythm table, computed from the
    release's inverse-kinematics trials;
  - scapular_muscles.json: the attachment points, wrap geometry, maximal forces (as PCSA) and fibre and tendon
    lengths of the trapezius, serratus anterior, rhomboids, levator scapulae, pectoralis minor and the rotator-cuff
    sub-regions;
  - build/references.py and the arm chain: the path points and the fibre and tendon lengths of the anterior and middle
    deltoid (DELT1, DELT2);
  - ligaments.json: strand lengths along the release's motions (which set the slack lengths); shoulder_strength.json:
    girdle postures taken from the release's motions.

  Seth A, Dong M, Matias R, Delp SL. Muscle contributions to upper-extremity movement and work from a
  musculoskeletal model of the human shoulder. Frontiers in Neurorobotics 13:90, 2019. doi:10.3389/fnbot.2019.00090
  Materials: SimTK project "thoracoscapular", https://simtk.org/projects/thoracoscapular,
  release FrontiersPubMaterials (ThoracoscapularShoulderPaperMaterials.zip).

Changes: values were converted into this package's body frames (scripts/fit_scapulothoracic.py); the
scapulothoracic joint is represented by MuJoCo joints and a contact rather than Seth's ellipsoid joint; the clavicle
gains an axial rotation and the girdle ligament strands, which the release does not have; the ellipsoidal thorax wraps
are replaced by fitted spheres; the levator scapulae is split into two slips and the clavicular trapezius gains an
occipital line; the rotator-cuff forces and fibre lengths follow Ward et al. 2006, and all forces follow this
package's shoulder specific tension (scripts/generate_scapular_muscles.py, scripts/fit_shoulder_strength.py). No file of
the release is redistributed. The authors do not endorse this model.
