Shoulder geometry and data from Seth et al. 2019

The scapulothoracic geometry in scapulothoracic.json (thoracic ellipsoid, scapular joint point, acromioclavicular
points, sternoclavicular axes) and the values derived from it in the arm and torso files are derived from:

  Seth A, Dong M, Matias R, Delp SL. Muscle contributions to upper-extremity movement and work from a
  musculoskeletal model of the human shoulder. Frontiers in Neurorobotics 13:90, 2019. doi:10.3389/fnbot.2019.00090
  Materials: SimTK project "thoracoscapular", https://simtk.org/projects/thoracoscapular,
  release FrontiersPubMaterials (ThoracoscapularShoulderPaperMaterials.zip).

Changes: values were converted into this package's body frames (scripts/fit_scapulothoracic.py); the
scapulothoracic joint is represented by MuJoCo joints and a contact rather than Seth's ellipsoid joint. No file of
the release is redistributed. The authors do not endorse this model.
