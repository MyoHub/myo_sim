Third-party data: University of Bath running dataset

Used in: bath_running.xml (marker names and placement descriptions; positions of the myomarker_fitted sites) and
myo_sim/build/data/markersets_bath.json

Source: Carter, J., Chen, X., Cazzola, D., Trewartha, G., Preatoni, E. (2024). Full Body Kinematics and Ground Reaction
Forces of Fifty Heterogeneous Runners Completing Treadmill Running at Various Speeds and Gradients. Bath: University of
Bath Research Data Archive. https://doi.org/10.15125/BATH-01341

Marker names and placement descriptions are taken from the dataset's MarkerSet_Info.docx. No recorded data is
included in this package.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: positions are medians of static trials mapped by per-segment similarity fits (modified). For each marker,
the median over the participants' static standing trials, mapped into the model by per-segment similarity fits on the
anatomical markers that sit on model landmark sites; generated by scripts/fit_markersets_bath.py from the published
files.
