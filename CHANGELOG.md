# Changelog

Model changes that alter actuators, joints, sites or dynamics are breaking for downstream environments and trained policies. List them here; they ship together in the next release tag.

## Unreleased

- **Side suffix on every right-arm name (all models with an arm or hand); names only, numerics identical.** In the right-arm source (and so in `myoarm_r`, `myoarms`, `myotorso_arms`, `myotorso_arm_r`, `myofullbody`):
  - **Joints:** `cmc_flexion`, `cmc_abduction`, `md3_flexion`, `pm4_flexion` -> `cmc_flexion_r`, `cmc_abduction_r`, `md3_flexion_r`, `pm4_flexion_r` (the hand models already used these names).
  - **Actuators:** all 66 right-arm muscles get `_r` (`DELT1` -> `DELT1_r`, ..., `OP` -> `OP_r`, `AdP` -> `AdP_r`), so a muscle has the same name in `myoarm_r` and `myohand_r`. Left-arm and hand-model actuator names are unchanged.
  - **Tendons:** muscle tendons are named after their actuator, as in the legs and torso: `OP_tendon` -> `OP_r_tendon` and, on the left, `OP_tendon_l` -> `OP_l_tendon` (new mirror rule `_r_tendon` -> `_l_tendon`). In the hand models `OP_tendon_r` -> `OP_r_tendon` and `OP_tendon_l` -> `OP_l_tendon`. The glenohumeral ligaments get `_r` (`s_glenohum_ligament_r`).
  - **Geoms:** the 89 unsuffixed right-arm geoms get `_r` (wrap geoms such as `2ndmcp_ellipsoid_wrap_r`, bone meshes `capitate_r`, `thumbprox_r`, `5distph_r`); 33 of them are in the hand models. Left names are unchanged.
  - **Cameras:** `hand_top`, `hand_bottom`, `hand_side_inter`, `hand_side_exter` get `_r`; the misspelt `plam_lookat` becomes `palm_lookat_r` (left: `palm_lookat_l`).
  - Every `MjModel` array and the tendon lengths, actuator lengths and moments, passive forces and body positions at five random postures are identical before and after for `myohand_r`, `myohands`, `myoarm_r`, `myoarms`, `myotorso_arms`, `myofullbody` and both hand fragments (`scripts/compare_model_numerics.py`). Joint, actuator and tendon order are unchanged, so action and observation layouts are unchanged.
  - **Downstream (MyoSuite `origin/dev`, to update when it moves to this release; nothing changed there yet):** actuator-name keys for arm-based models in `myosuite/core/muscle_groups.py`, the muscle lists of `myosuite/integrations/musclemimic/bimanual_model.py` and `model_bridge.py`, `myosuite/tests/test_tabletennis_cpu.py` (`EIP`, `EPL`) and `myosuite/tests/test_coverage_gaps.py`; the joint names in `myosuite/core/model_recipes.py` (docstring), `integrations/musclemimic/bimanual_model.py` (`_translate_finger_token` already accepts both forms), `myosuite/tests/test_hand_recipe_parity.py`, `test_model_builder.py`, `test_myoedits_arm.py`, `docs/source/model_builder.rst` and the column names of `tutorials/files/3.3/trajectory.csv`; the camera name in `tutorials/files/2.3/sar_tutorial_utils.py` (`hand_top`, with a fallback); code that strips `_tendon` from tendon names to get muscle names (`envs/myo/backends/mjlab/register_mjlab_tasks.py`) now gets the actuator name for arm and hand models too, and `.*_tendon$` patterns now match arm and hand muscle tendons. DynSyn group files, synergy and pose recipes and checkpoints that store arm actuator or tendon names need the new names; checkpoints themselves are unaffected (same order and dynamics).
- **Hand models derived from the arm by generic pruning (`myohand_r`, `myohands`, hand fragments).** `build/hand.py` now renames every reference generically (`rename_elements`: actuators, equalities, sensors, pairs, excludes, cameras; tendons re-created in their original order) and removes whatever names a removed element. The hand models' contact pairs are the arm's pairs (`myoarm_contacts.xml`) that survive pruning, plus the finger pairs; `myohand_contacts.xml` keeps only the finger pairs. Numerics of `myohand_r` and of the bare hand fragments are unchanged; their contact-pair names now follow `myoarm_contacts.xml` (e.g. `radius_coll_r_thorax_coll1_1`). `myohands` gains the 12 forearm pairs of `myoarms` between one arm's humerus or ulna and the other arm's radius (53 pairs, was 41). No arm model changes. Downstream: none expected (no observation or action change); code that looks up hand contact pairs by name must use the new names.
- **Head and trunk mass (all models with the torso chain).** The head body now carries 5.848 kg (6.94 % of body mass, de Leva 1996) and the torso 14.897 kg instead of 18.619 kg; the total of `myofullbody` is unchanged at 84.26 kg and the whole-body centre of mass rises by 1.4 cm. The OpenSim torso mass already contained the head, so the head was previously about 2.5 % of body mass. Downstream: dynamics of every model with the torso and head change slightly; policies trained on the old masses (e.g. MyoSuite and MuscleMimic `myofullbody` checkpoints) should be re-evaluated or retrained, and MyoSuite parity baselines regenerated when it moves to this release.
