# Changelog

Model changes that alter actuators, joints, sites or dynamics are breaking for downstream environments and trained policies. List them here; they ship together in the next release tag.

- **Renamed:** `_r` on every right-arm joint, actuator, tendon, geom and camera; `AdP` -> `AdPt`, `FPB` -> `FPBs`, `RI2` -> `DI1d`, `UI_UB5` -> `ADM`, `LU_RB2-5` -> `LUM1-4`, `UI_UB2` -> `PI1`, `RI3` -> `DI2`, `UI_UB3` -> `DI3`, `RI4` -> `PI2`, `UI_UB4` -> `DI4`, `RI5` -> `PI3`.
- **New actuators:** per hand `AdPo`, `FPBd`, `DI1s`, `FDM`, `ODM`; per leg `pect`, `quad_fem`, `gem`; in `myofullbody` the 72 neck muscles, last.
- **New joints:** per hand `cmc4_flexion`, `cmc5_flexion`, `node_LUM1-4`, `node_JT3-5`; in `myofullbody` 24 neck joints after the lumbar joints, so every arm and leg index moves. Build joint lists by name; filter `node_` joints and `lig_`, `node_`, `passive_` tendons from observations.
- **Dynamics:** `vmax` 10 on the forearm and hand muscles makes hand movement 2-5 times faster for the same activation; rigid-tendon maps change leg and upper-arm fibre lengths and forces. All hand, arm, leg and full-body checkpoints need retraining and parity baselines regenerating. mujoco-warp needs `nconmax` >= 64 for hand models.

- **Knee joint centre (`myolegs`, `myolegs_abdomen`, `myofullbody`).** The three knee hinges of each leg now sit at Rajagopal's walker_knee joint frame instead of the tibia's origin. Seen from the tibia, the femur's origin was 4.80, 12.75 and 15.53 mm off OpenSim's at 30, 90 and 120 deg of flexion and now matches it within 0.0007 mm at 0, 30, 90 and 120 deg. Downstream: the knee's kinematics change, and so may the moment arms of the muscles crossing it; policies trained on the old knee should be re-evaluated or retrained.

- **`LTpT_T12_l` path (all models with the torso chain).** Five path points of the left T12 longissimus had the right muscle's coordinates, so it seems to have run down the right side of the spine; their z is now negated. Downstream: at qpos = 0, with every other coordinate held fixed, this muscle's moment arm about `lat_bending` goes from +26.86 mm to -26.86 mm, so policies trained on the old path should be re-evaluated or retrained.

- **Pelvis counted once (`myofullbody`, `myolegs`, `myolegs26`, `myolegs_abdomen`).** The torso chain's sacrum is now massless, so the pelvis mass is the leg chain's alone; `myofullbody` weighs 76.78 kg instead of 84.26 kg, and the head (unchanged) is 7.6 % of it. Downstream: dynamics of the four models with legs change; policies trained on them should be re-evaluated or retrained. Torso-only models only lose 7.486 kg of reported mass, since their sacrum is welded to the world.

- **CMC4/5 springs, ORL, EDC2's wrist sphere (all models with a hand).** See `docs/wiki/hand-cmc-joints.md`, `extensor-mechanism.md` and `hand-literature-comparison.md` (cupping and ORL step).

- **One specific tension for the wrist and hand muscles (all models with an arm or hand).** See `docs/wiki/hand-function-validation.md` and `hand-literature-comparison.md` (specific-tension step).

- **Numerical stability at the 2 ms Euler step: `vmax` and thumb CMC armature (all models with an arm).** See `docs/wiki/hand-literature-comparison.md` (*Force-velocity damping*).

Moved through dynamics or contact friction: IP coupling slope at PIP 70 deg 0.25 -> 0.37, middle-finger junctura share 17.7 -> 16.1 deg. Step time unchanged. No actuator, joint, site or tendon added, removed or renamed.
  - **MyoSuite:** hand and forearm movement is 2-5 times faster for the same activation (peak free speed, wrist / thumb CMC / middle-finger MCP: 3.9 / 4.5 / 3.7 -> 14.3 / 12.2 / 18.6 rad/s; 6.5 / 6.8 / 9.1 on `dev`), so policies trained on any earlier model need retraining, and task timing and reward scales that assume the slower hand need checking.
